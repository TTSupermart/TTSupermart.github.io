Operations Calendar Sync

This private tool connects the T&T Supermart Operations Calendar & Tasks app on an authorized Windows computer to a dedicated Google Calendar. It lets authorized staff view and edit task dates and recurring schedules in Google Calendar, including on a phone.

Calendar changes are checked when the Operations app is open and when staff next open it. The app uses Google Calendar access only for its designated Operations calendar.

Read the Operations Calendar privacy notice.
