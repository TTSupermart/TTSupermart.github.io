Website privacy

We use optional Google Analytics statistics to understand how people find and use this website. Analytics remains off unless you choose “Allow analytics.”

What is measured

  • Page views, visits, general engagement, and approximate location.
  • Browser, device type, screen size, and referring website.
  • Useful actions such as opening the weekly ad, calling, emailing, using social links, and navigating to store information.

What we avoid

We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or the contents of messages to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in our website configuration.

Your choice

You can allow or decline analytics when first visiting the site. You can change that choice at any time below. Declining removes analytics cookies created for this website where the browser permits it.
