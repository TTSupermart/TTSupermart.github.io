Operations Calendar Sync privacy

The T&T Supermart Operations Calendar Sync is for authorized staff. It connects the local Operations Calendar & Tasks app to a dedicated Google Calendar owned by an authorized account.

Google data used

With the account owner's consent, the app reads, creates, changes, and deletes events in the designated Operations calendar. It exchanges task titles, descriptions, start and end dates or times, and repeating schedules. Google grants access to events on calendars the account owns, but the app is configured to use only the designated Operations calendar. The account email address is checked to make sure the correct account is connected.

Storage and use

Operations tasks and sync links remain in the app's local database. Google authorization credentials are encrypted for the Windows account running the app. Calendar event data is sent to Google Calendar to provide two-way synchronization. The app does not use Google Calendar data for advertising or sell it to others. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

An authorized user can disconnect Google Calendar in the Operations app, which removes the locally stored authorization. They can also revoke access in their Google Account settings. Disconnecting does not delete existing tasks in the app or events already synchronized to Google Calendar; those can be deleted separately in the respective app.
